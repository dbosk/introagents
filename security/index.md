# LLMs, agents and security: who checks?

This section holds a two-hour guest lecture on language models, AI agents
and security, written for students in a first programming course.

The lecture asks one question of everything a language model produces: who
checks? It tests the question on the proofs of the Riemann hypothesis that
were announced in the autumn of 2026, and then on security: first for a
company that builds a language model into a product, and then for your own
use of AI agents.

The material is built from one source into slides and lecture notes. The
notes include a customer-support chatbot that is vulnerable by design, with
its complete source and instructions for running it, so that you can try
attacking it on your own computer.

- [The chatbot's source in `security/chatbot/`](https://github.com/dbosk/introagents/tree/main/security/chatbot)
